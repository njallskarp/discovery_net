# Geometric and Topological Approach

Recast the target in a space where shape, continuity, incidence, dimension, or topology supplies the obstruction or construction.

Keep the geometric mechanism independent of implementation. Use suitable `$math-tool-*` skills for visualization, research code, computer algebra, or rigorous numerics when those capabilities are needed.

## Working mode

- Identify the natural space, topology, metric, complex, polytope, moduli object, or geometric realization.
- State invariance under coordinates, normalization, and symmetry.
- Look for compactness, convexity, deformation, intersection, degree, homology, curvature, or local-to-global mechanisms.
- Check degeneracies, boundary strata, singular cases, orientation, and dimension assumptions.
- Use visualization or computation to discover geometry, but prove the invariant or obstruction independently when possible.
- When translating a discrete problem into geometry, prove both directions of the correspondence needed for the theorem.

Prefer a geometric mechanism explaining an entire family to a plotted pattern or numerical embedding.

## Pivot condition

If the geometric model loses essential combinatorial information, identify the nonfaithful step and either enrich the model or return a precise obstruction to the selector.

Report the geometric object, correspondence, invariant, exceptional strata, and any unproved translation bridge.
