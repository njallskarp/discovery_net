# Algebraic Structure Approach

Make the result flow from structure rather than case enumeration.

## Working mode

- Identify the natural algebraic object and the action, homomorphism, module, ideal, or factorization governing the problem.
- Quotient by symmetry only after proving that the quotient preserves the target property and multiplicities.
- Search for invariants, canonical forms, decompositions, orbit-stabilizer consequences, rank formulas, and local-to-global algebraic bridges.
- Separate coordinate artifacts from basis-independent statements.
- State exceptional characteristics, singular parameters, and failure of semisimplicity or invertibility explicitly.
- Use computation to discover identities or verify finite exceptional cases, then seek a uniform structural proof.

Prefer a theorem explaining an observed spectrum or census to extending that census by another parameter.

## Pivot condition

If the proposed structure does not constrain the target, prove the failure with a counterexample or dimension calculation and identify the missing invariant.

Report the algebraic objects, maps, decomposition, structural lemma, exceptional cases, and any computational bridge still outside the proof.
